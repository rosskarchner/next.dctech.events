Spot something wrong with an event?

No account needed — corrections go through the same email-a-link flow as submissions.

1. Find the event's page on this site. On any listing — the homepage, a category, a week — an event's title links out to the organizer's own site, not here. Look instead for the small magnifying glass right after the title; that's the link to this site's own page for the event.

2. On the event's page, click "Report an issue with this event." It's in the links row near the bottom, next to "Email us directly."

3. Enter your email and request a correction link. We use it to send the link and to follow up if we have questions — it's never published.

4. Click the link we email you. It opens a form pre-filled with the event's current details. Fill in what's wrong and how you know, and a moderator will review it. Prefer email instead? Write to ross@karchner.com.
